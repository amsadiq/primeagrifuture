About Us

An agricultural system built for quality, efficiency and sustainability.

Prime AgriFuture manages an agricultural system that produces high-quality farm produce and livestock, alongside the timely and efficient procurement and distribution of machinery and cutting-edge technologies to ensure maximum farm outputs.

Nigeria faces a significant protein deficit, yet livestock farming remains dominated by open grazing — driving deforestation, land degradation and farmer–herder conflict. We take a different path: a zero-grazing, veterinary-led production model that raises animals in controlled enclosures while actively regenerating the land around them.

Our Model

A zero-grazing, circular ecosystem.

We eliminate open grazing entirely. Every part of the system feeds the next — reducing pressure on land while keeping animals productive year-round.

01

Controlled Enclosures

Animals are raised in managed pens rather than roaming freely — removing land competition and the conflict and degradation it causes.

02

Tree Planting

A tree-planting program across the ranch restores vegetation and improves carbon capture on previously strained land.

03

Circular Nutrients

Animal waste becomes organic manure for crop farmers; in return we collect crop by-products to produce sustainable feed.

04

Year-Round Feed

We produce local hay and silage so animals are fed consistently through every season, easing pressure on natural ecosystems.
